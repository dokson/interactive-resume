Professional Summary

Alessandro Colace is a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) and Cloud Data & AI Architect with 15+ years shipping backend, platform and data systems end to end, in tech since high school. He runs COalesCE, his own independent technology consultancy in Milan, Italy: embedded with the client's own teams, he takes AI, data, legacy systems and security all the way into production, with no hand-offs. COalesCE spans cloud data platforms, AI in production, legacy modernisation, cybersecurity, custom software and technical governance, for SMEs and data-intensive enterprises alike.

He learned the trade in data and AI consulting, at Data Reply, Go Reply, The Information LAB, Nimbus Intelligence, Iconsulting, and Avatar Investments: building modern data platforms from scratch (warehouses and lakehouses, pipelines, governance) and leading data teams on enterprise engagements for major Italian clients such as Unipol, Yoox Net-a-Porter (YNAP), Telepass, Campari, Ferragamo, Fedrigoni, and IEO.

His work covers the whole path from data to decisions: data platforms that become the company's single source of truth, AI and agents grounded in its own data and processes and taken into production, and the governance that keeps both secure, compliant and trustworthy over time.

Also available as a Fractional CTO / CDO / CIO: a part-time technology executive who steps in for platform choice, vendor contracts and architecture review. Holds a Master's degree in Computer Engineering from Università di Bologna, Summa Cum Laude (GPA 4.0/4.0).
