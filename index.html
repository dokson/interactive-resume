About

Alessandro Colace is a Milan-based AI Engineer, Data Engineer, and Forward Deployed Engineer with over fifteen years of experience in enterprise data platforms, cloud architecture, and AI transformation. He is the founder of COalesCE (coalesce.coach), an independent technology consultancy based in Milan, delivered end to end by one person on the Forward Deployed Engineer model. Its services span Data Foundations (data engineering, cloud data platforms, reporting), AI in production (AI adoption audits, AI platform deployment including Palantir AIP and Foundry, Agentic AI automation), Software Rescue and legacy modernisation, Cybersecurity for SMEs, custom web and mobile applications, Fractional CTO/CDO/CIO governance, and technical training. His core expertise spans Palantir AIP, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Large Language Models (LLM), Agentic AI systems, MLOps, LLMOps, and end-to-end data platform engineering on Google Cloud Platform (GCP), Amazon Web Services (AWS), and Microsoft Azure. He holds six professional certifications: four Google Cloud Professional certifications (Machine Learning Engineer, Network Engineer, DevOps Engineer, and Database Engineer), a Six Sigma Yellow Belt, and Tableau Desktop Specialist. Previously he served as Big Data Architect and Data Engineering team lead managing multi-million euro business development across major consulting engagements, and as Operations Manager leading the turnaround of a manufacturing company in Ukraine. He is a Master's graduate in Computer Engineering from the Università di Bologna, Summa Cum Laude. Alessandro is available for AI engineering, data architecture, and enterprise AI transformation engagements in Milan and remotely across Italy and Europe.
